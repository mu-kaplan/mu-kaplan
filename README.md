## Featured Projects

### 🛒 [Olist E-Commerce Analytics](https://github.com/mu-kaplan/olist-ecommerce-analysis)

End-to-end analysis of **99,441 Brazilian e-commerce orders**, covering sales growth, customer behavior, product and seller performance, delivery operations, and geographic logistics.

**Highlights**

- Built a reproducible Python → DuckDB → SQL → Power BI analytics workflow.
- Designed separate order- and item-level fact tables to preserve analytical grain.
- Developed a five-page interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Found that only ~3% of delivered customers repeated during the observed period.
- Identified a rise in negative reviews from 9.22% for early deliveries to 62.42% for late deliveries.
- Found interstate single-seller orders had ~91% longer delivery times than same-state orders.
- Produced a business report with prioritized operational recommendations.

### 🌍 [Leeds Flood Exposure GIS](https://github.com/mu-kaplan/leeds-flood-exposure-gis)

QGIS analysis of **building, road, and selected facility exposure to mapped flood zones in Leeds**, combining Environment Agency flood zones, OpenStreetMap infrastructure, ONS boundaries, and OS Terrain 50 elevation data.

**Highlights**

- Prepared spatial datasets using projected coordinate systems, geometry validation, and repair.
- Identified **7,868 building features** with positive-area overlap with the combined flood zones, counting features overlapping both categories once.
- Measured **255.20 km of overlapping road geometry**, approximately **5.35%** of the selected road network.
- Reported **60 facility polygon records** and **12 facility point records** separately to avoid implying a count of unique institutions.
- Tested how polygon overlap thresholds affect building and facility counts.
- Derived slope and hillshade from elevation data and calculated terrain statistics within flood zones.
- Created three maps with PNG and PDF exports, supported by source documentation and a processing log.

**Interpretation:** Results describe spatial overlap with mapped flood zones, not confirmed flooding, damage, or individual-property risk.
